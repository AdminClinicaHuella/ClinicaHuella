LEADER grant

CLÍNICA HUELLA, S.L., with its project «HUELLA», has been the beneficiary of a grant for the creation of businesses carrying out non-agricultural activities in rural areas, under the LEADER scheme included in Spain’s Strategic Plan for the Common Agricultural Policy (PEPAC) 2023-2027, within the Comunitat Valenciana, for an amount of €100,000.
