About HUELLA

This project was born out of a vocation to care for and accompany those we love most, inspired by all those who have left their mark on our journey.

With that same philosophy, we have created every corner of our center with the utmost care and attention to detail, thinking of the wellbeing of our patients and the comfort of their families.

We have Cat Friendly facilities, waiting rooms and consultation rooms adapted to the needs of each species, plus a room specially designed to say goodbye to our companions with intimacy, respect and love.

Meet our team

Víctor Zomeño

Veterinarian and co-founder of Clínica Huella

He has experience in small animal practice, surgery, and emergency care, as well as having held leadership and team management positions. These experiences have allowed him to develop a broad vision of the profession and reaffirm one of his main convictions: to offer high-quality medicine without ever losing sight of the personal and human touch.

From childhood, he knew he wanted to dedicate his life to the care of animals. This vocation led him to study Veterinary Medicine, and since then, he has been fortunate enough to turn his passion into his profession.

His main areas of interest are internal medicine and surgery, especially diagnosis and finding solutions that improve the quality of life of his patients. Committed to continuing education, he regularly participates in conferences, courses, and workshops to stay up-to-date with the latest advances.

He shares his life with Kuma, his four-legged companion and a constant source of inspiration to continue practicing with passion and commitment.

Cristina Listón

Veterinary nurse and co-founder of Clínica Huella

She has over seven years of experience in leading veterinary clinics and hospitals, where she has worked in different areas of veterinary medicine.

Her main interests are oncology, emergency care and Cat Friendly handling, always with the aim of providing care that minimises stress and supports the well-being of every patient. She also pursues continuous training by attending congresses, courses and conferences, and takes part in scientific projects and publications.

Committed to animal welfare, she actively collaborates with animal shelters and takes part in caring for animals in vulnerable situations.

She shares her life with Antia, her four-legged companion and one of the reasons this project exists.

At the clinic, she will be the one to welcome you and accompany you, making sure that both you and your four-legged companions receive close, safe and quality care.
